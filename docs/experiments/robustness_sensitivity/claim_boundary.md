# Claim boundary

The tables in this package describe outputs of the frozen V2 scientific engine for the frozen 179-unit reference project under preregistered input perturbations. They establish computational behavior and sensitivity inside that model.

They do not establish official water allocation, measured water use, reservoir availability, real-farm profitability, market absorption, agronomic prescriptions, policy recommendations or real-world feasibility. The critical water multiplier is a structural threshold derived from the model's protected-perennial floor and engine budget. Price and yield sensitivity are not claimed because those fields are not independent decision inputs on the frozen execution path.
