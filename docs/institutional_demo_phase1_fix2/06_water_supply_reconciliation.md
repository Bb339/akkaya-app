# Annual/monthly supply reconciliation

The normal verified pair represents one planning-calendar-year supply concept. The annual value must equal the sum of its twelve planning-year monthly rows within `max(1 m3, 0.1% of annual supply)`. Exact and boundary values pass; a larger difference blocks readiness and execution with the annual value, monthly total, and tolerance in the error.

Dataset replacement remains versioned and preview-pinned. No source is silently preferred when a material contradiction exists.
