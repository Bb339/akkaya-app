# Calendar-year water contract

Demand keys use the date being evaluated: `YYYY-MM` comes from `current.year` and `current.month`. A 2024-10-01 through 2025-06-30 season therefore retains 2024-10, 2024-11, 2024-12, and 2025-01 through 2025-06.

Monthly supply and delivery imports must contain all twelve planning-year months. They may additionally contain months from the immediately preceding year for a cross-year crop season. Supply and delivery must have identical period sets. The annual `m3/year` record reconciles to the twelve planning-year monthly supply rows; preceding-year rows serve their matching calendar-period validation and are not relabelled or counted in the planning-year annual allocation.
