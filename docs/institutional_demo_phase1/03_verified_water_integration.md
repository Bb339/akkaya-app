# Verified water integration

`resolve_verified_water` selects the active confirmed versions of annual supply, 12-month supply, 12-month delivery capacity, environmental release, conveyance efficiency and perennial requirements for the project planning year. Eligible authorities are `MEASURED`, `OFFICIAL_ALLOCATION` and `OFFICIAL_HISTORICAL`.

The adapter pins IDs, versions, authorities and sources. Annual supply becomes the copied bundle budget; monthly supply and delivery become engine seasonal resources; one verified conveyance efficiency updates copied irrigation records; perennial requirements update matching S2 rows. The frozen engine accepts an annual environmental-release ratio. Physical monthly release remains preserved by the data contract but fails closed for execution because no equivalent frozen-engine input exists.

Reference-mode assumptions remain unchanged and visibly labelled as demo evidence.
